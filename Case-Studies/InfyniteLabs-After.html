Development timeline

From idea to a market-ready MVP in just 5 weeks.

Here’s how it works.

A call will be scheduled to discuss the idea, business goals, and core features, followed by market validation of the concept.

Wireframes, user flows, designs, necessary tools, and PRDs are created, followed by the preparation of a detailed proposal for the MVP.

Upon proposal approval, development of the MVP will begin, with regular updates provided over the next 4–5 weeks.

Once the MVP is complete, the market-ready product will be delivered to you for launch.

Four weeks of free support will be provided to address any technical issues, critical bug fixes and optimizations.
